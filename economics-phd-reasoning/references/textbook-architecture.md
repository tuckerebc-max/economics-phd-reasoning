# `OPT-67-ECONOMICS` textbook-skill architecture

**Architecture ID:** `F2-ARCH-67-ECONOMICS-001`  
**Status:** `SPEC_DRAFT — FIELD VARIATION VISIBLE`  
**Promise:** Teach advanced economic research reasoning: models, assumptions, identification, evidence, robustness, interpretation, and defense.

## Learner change

Before: the learner presents a model, regression, or result as self-explanatory.  
After: the learner states a contribution, interrogates assumptions, aligns methods to claims, stress-tests evidence, and defends interpretation.

## Progression

`Question → Model → Evidence → Estimate / reason → Stress-test → Interpret → Defend → Revise`

## Unit map

| Unit | Work | Learner evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question, literature, and contribution | research-position memo |
| 2 | Model construction and assumptions | model / assumptions audit |
| 3 | Identification and method choice | design / identification memo |
| 4 | Analysis, robustness, and alternatives | replication / robustness packet |
| 5 | Interpretation and policy meaning | results / implications memo |
| 6 | Seminar, peer review, and dissertation defense | defense packet and revision log |

## Source boundary

QAA and AEA provide field-level context; Stanford Economics is a university-specific exemplar. No single universal Economics PhD competency or scoring standard is claimed.

## Human-plus-agent design

The agent may check algebra, code, assumptions, and alternative specifications. The human defines the research contribution, decides whether evidence supports the claim, and interprets substantive meaning.

## Evaluator seed

Dimensions: question-method fit, model transparency, identification, evidence quality, robustness, uncertainty, interpretation, response to critique, and reproducibility.

## First drafting packet

Draft `ECON-001` will challenge a plausible empirical claim with a hidden identification problem. The learner must diagnose the problem, propose a bounded repair, and state what remains unidentifiable.
