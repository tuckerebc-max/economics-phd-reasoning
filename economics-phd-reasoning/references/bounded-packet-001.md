## `OPT-67-ECONOMICS` — Diagnose an identification problem

**Learner target:** Match a research claim to assumptions, identification, evidence, and robustness.

**Fictional study:** A researcher compares outcomes in places that adopted a policy earlier with outcomes in places that adopted later. The groups differ before the policy, but the draft interprets the difference as a causal effect.

**Learner task:** State the claim type, identify assumptions, diagnose the identification problem, propose a bounded design repair, and state what remains unidentifiable.

**Required artifact:** contribution memo, assumptions audit, identification review, alternative explanations, robustness plan, narrowed conclusion.

**Nonmodel:** “The coefficient is statistically significant, so the policy caused the difference.”  
**Feedback move:** Ask “What would have to be true for the comparison to identify the causal effect, and what evidence tests that assumption?”

**Agent handoff:** Agent can check calculations and propose specifications; researcher owns the substantive claim and interpretation.

**Exit test:** Provide a plausible robustness check that does not address the core confounding. Learner must explain why it is insufficient.
